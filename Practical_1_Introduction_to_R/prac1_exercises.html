Character, Numerical, and Logical Variables:

char = "hello!"
num = 220
log = TRUE

print(char)
[1] "hello!"
print(class(char))
[1] "character"
print(num)
[1] 220
print(class(num))
[1] "numeric"
print(log)
[1] TRUE
print(class(log))
[1] "logical"
